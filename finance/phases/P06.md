## What shipped

Pure TypeScript in `app/src/engine/`. No IndexedDB, no React. The phone UI is unchanged from Phase 1.

- **`freeToAllocate(books)`** — liquid − budget reserved − committed cash, plus breakdown lines
- **`nextMonthEstimate(books)`** — free today − next month Loan/EMI + **settings salary** − next month cap
- **`forecast(books, 6)`** — current month + next 5; each card has Loan/EMI · Lifestyle · Investment · contributing plan rows · projected liquid
- **`projectedLiquid(previous, flow)`** — the per-month step
- **`recurringDueLines` / `oneTimeInMonth`** — so a Forecast card can answer “which rows make up Loan/EMI in Nov?”

Formulas match architecture §5.5–5.7 and the live sheet after the Sep 2026 free-cash fix:

```
budget_reserved = max(0, budget remaining)
committed       = CC due + remaining EMI this month + Planned one-time (30 d)
free            = liquid − budget_reserved − committed
est_next        = free − next month Loan/EMI + settings.monthly_salary − next cap
```

- Negative free cash is allowed (and required on the frozen Sep-2026 snapshot).
- Lifestyle recurring that already sits inside the budget cap is **not** subtracted again.
- Expected inflows are listed (`expectedInflowsIfReceived`), not added to free.
- Next-month does **not** subtract CC again (already inside today’s free).
- Next-month salary is the settings value, never last ledger income.

Forecast:

- First month is **this month** (same as Planned Expenses `L6`).
- Month cards split `recurringDue` by kind and attach `lines` (plan id, name, kind, amount).
- Projected liquid starts at today’s liquid. **This month:** no salary add; subtract remaining budget, remaining EMI, remaining one-times. **Later months:** + settings salary − that month’s cap − Loan/EMI − investment recurring − one-times in the month.
- Lifestyle is shown on the card but **not** subtracted from projected liquid (it lives inside the cap). Architecture §5.7 wrote `recurring_due(m,*)`; the frozen double-count rule wins.
- Toggle **assume inflows arrive** defaults **off**. When on, only `status=expected` **liquid** inflows in that month are added.
