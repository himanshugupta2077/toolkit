# Phase 2 — Types, money, ledger validation (done, awaiting your review)

Implemented 2026-09-06. Product checkbox in `plan.md` stays unticked until you approve.

## What shipped

Pure TypeScript in `app/src/engine/`. No IndexedDB, no React, no keypad. The phone UI is unchanged from Phase 1.

- **Money** is integer paise (`₹ × 100`). `rupeesToPaise(1150.5)` → `115050`; `formatInr` uses Indian grouping (`₹1,40,000.00`).
- **Domain types:** account, category, ledger entry, `YYYY-MM` month, IST civil dates (`Asia/Kolkata`).
- **`validateLedgerEntry`** — Type Guide from `01` §4.2 / the sheet:
  - amount > 0, from ≠ to
  - income: from is virtual Employer or External
  - expense: to is virtual Expense
  - cc_payment: to is a credit-card liability
  - investment: to group is `fd` or `investment`
  - adjustment: category Reconciliation
  - transfer: both sides are real accounts
  - refund: from is virtual Expense
