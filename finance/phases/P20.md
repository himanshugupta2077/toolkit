## What shipped

- **Goals list** (`/wealth/goals`) — sorted by priority, Up/Down reorder, funded ring, status pills from the Phase 8 engine (*Affordable now* / *On track* / *Behind* / *Saving* / *Achieved*). Header shows total remaining and “Funded from Savings buffer”.
- **Add / edit** — name, target ₹, optional date, funding bucket (default Savings), notes. Blank target is allowed so imported goals can sit until you fill a number.
- **Detail** (`/wealth/goals/:id`) — remaining hero, “You could fund this today: yes/no and why” (bucket balance minus higher-priority remaining), contributions, Pause / Resume / Mark achieved.
- **Fund now** — opens Quick Add as a **Transfer**, amount prefilled, `goalId` on the ledger row. Saving writes a contribution linked to that row. Hitting the target sets **Achieved**.
- **Add contribution** — no bank move; a **note is required**.
- **Wealth strip** — next 3 active goals with pills, plus See all.
- Blank-target imported goals show **Fill a target amount** on the card and detail.

Approval cases:

- Savings ₹10,000 + a ₹8,000 goal → Affordable now; a second ₹8,000 goal is not (₹2,000 left after the first).
- Funding ₹8,000 against an ₹8,000 target writes a contribution and the pill becomes Achieved.
