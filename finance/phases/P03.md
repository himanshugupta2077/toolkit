## What shipped

Pure TypeScript in `app/src/engine/`. No IndexedDB, no React. The phone UI is unchanged from Phase 1.

- **Asset balance** = opening + Σ to − Σ from
- **Liability due** = opening + Σ from − Σ to
- **Liquid** = Σ calculated balance where `includeLiquid` (same SUMIFS as the sheet)
- **CC due / available / utilisation** — cards only (`liability` + `credit_card`); available = limit − due; utilisation = due / limit
- **Net worth** = Σ includeNetWorth assets − Σ includeNetWorth liabilities

Virtual accounts use the asset formula but stay out of liquid and net worth unless you flip those flags. Opening is always applied; an optional `asOf` date only filters ledger rows (inclusive). Omit `asOf` to include every row, which is how Reconciliation works. Holdings NAV is not applied (Phase 22).
