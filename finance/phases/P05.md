## What shipped

Pure TypeScript in `app/src/engine/`. No IndexedDB, no React. The phone UI is unchanged from Phase 1.

- **`recurringDue(month, kind, plans, categories)`** — active + start/end overlap; `kind: null` = all kinds
- **`oneTimeWindow(days, today, plans)`** — status Planned only, `[today, today+days]` inclusive
- **`committedEmiRemaining(month, plans, categories, entries)`** — Loan/EMI due − ledger category EMIs, floor 0
- **`resolveRecurringKind`** — blank Kind infers EMIs → `loan_emi`, Investment → `investment`, else `lifestyle`

Formulas match architecture §5.4 and the Planned Expenses sheet helpers:

- Monthly: due if Active and Start ≤ month-end and (End blank or End ≥ month-start). Blank Start = already started. End on any day of the month still counts that month.
- Yearly: full amount in the **anniversary month only** (never ÷12). Blank Start → 0. Does not count before the start year.
- Weekly / every-N-months are in the type (architecture §4.2) and due in their occurrence months.
- One-time window matches sheet B8/B9: `>= today` and `<= today+days`, status Planned. Past Planned, Completed, and Cancelled are excluded.
- Remaining EMI = `max(0, recurringDue(loan_emi) − Monthly Budget B12 EMIs)`.
