## What shipped

Home is the Simple Dashboard over SQLite. You still cannot type a balance; every number is calculated.

- **Header** — `September 2026 · 25 days left` (IST month).
- **Pace card** — Safe/day (or “No spending yet” when spent is 0), remaining of cap, used % vs elapsed %, two-tone bar (green / amber / red). Tap opens a Pace sheet: formula + top 5 in-budget categories → Ledger.
- **Action cards** (only when relevant) — unverified import → Accounts; stale / never reconciled → that account’s Reconcile; negative free cash (“Committed beyond liquid”); Allocate is a dead button until Phase 19 when free is positive.
- **Credit cards** — one row per live card (due, utilisation, due-in). Tap → account detail. Hidden if you have no cards.
- **Free to allocate** — big number (red when negative) + collapsible breakdown that matches the engine/sheet (liquid − budget reserved − CC − remaining EMI − one-time 30d) + grey “Expected inflows if received” + est. free next month with its own breakdown.
- **This month** 2×3 tiles — Income, Budget exp, Non-budget exp, Investments, EMIs + rent, Est. savings. Tiles open Ledger with a filter.
- **Next 6 months** compact stacked bars (Loan/EMI · Lifestyle · Investment). Tap → Plan (Forecast is Phase 16).
- **Recent** — last 5 ledger rows → detail; See all → Ledger.

`GET /api/home` is the payload (same `budgetPace` / `freeToAllocate` / `forecast` / `monthSummary` the engine already had). Quick Add, Ledger edits, and reconcile invalidate it.

Out of scope as planned: privacy blur / PWA (Phase 17), Plan tab (Phase 16), Allocate confirm (Phase 19), writing either Excel file, IndexedDB.
