## What shipped

The Plan tab is the planning surface over SQLite. **Planning never posts to the ledger.** Completing a one-time only flips status.

Sub-tabs: **Budget · Recurring · One-time · Inflows · Forecast** (`/plan?tab=`). Home’s 6-month strip opens **Forecast**.

- **Budget** — month switcher (last 12 → next 6), cap with Edit sheet + “apply to future months too”, spent / remaining / used vs elapsed, in-budget category bars → Ledger, month list (cap · income · budget exp · est. savings · pace dot).
- **Recurring** — monthly fixed cost, active count, yearly commitments (full amount, not ÷12). Kind chips including Inactive. Cards: name · amount · frequency · next due · pay-from · kind · Active switch. Ended plans fold under **Ended**. Add/edit sheet.
- **One-time** — next 30 / 90 / total planned. Planned · Completed · Cancelled. Complete / Cancel on a Planned card. Complete does **not** write a ledger row.
- **Inflows** — “Expected, not counted” + explainer. Empty state is fine.
- **Forecast** — assume-inflows toggle (off by default), stacked bars, 6 month cards with projected liquid (red if negative). Tap Loan/EMI · Lifestyle · Investment to see which rows make up that kind.

`GET /api/plan` is the payload. Writes: `PUT /api/budgets/:yyyymm`, `POST/PATCH /api/plans/recurring`, `/one-time`, `/inflows`. `GET /api/forecast` is the documented forecast endpoint. Turning a recurring row off updates Home remaining EMI / free cash on the next fetch.

Out of scope as planned: “mark paid → Quick Add” linking (Complete is status-only), swipe, privacy blur / PWA (Phase 17), Allocate confirm (Phase 19), writing either Excel file, IndexedDB.
